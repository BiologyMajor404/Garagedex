Contact us

Write to rizzlershrekizzler@gmail.com. In the app the same e-mail opens from Profile → About the app → Report a problem or suggest an idea. We reply as soon as we can.

The AI named the wrong car

You always confirm the result yourself: pick another suggestion or choose the car by hand from the catalog. If a car’s details in the catalog are wrong, open the car and tap Report an error in the data.

Premium, subscriptions and refunds

Premium is bought through the App Store or Google Play, and the store handles payment, renewal and refunds. You can cancel a subscription at any time: on iPhone in Settings → your name → Subscriptions, on Android in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. After cancelling, the subscription does not renew and Premium stays until the end of the paid period. Refunds are decided by the store: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, Google Play in your order history.

Premium, subskrypcje i zwroty

Premium kupuje się przez App Store lub Google Play, a płatności, odnowienia i zwroty obsługuje sklep. Subskrypcję możesz anulować w każdej chwili: na iPhonie w Ustawienia → Twoje imię → Subskrypcje, na Androidzie w Google Play → Płatności i subskrypcje → Subskrypcje. Po anulowaniu subskrypcja się nie odnawia, a Premium zostaje do końca opłaconego okresu. O zwrocie pieniędzy decyduje sklep: Apple na reportaproblem.apple.com, Google Play w historii zamówień.
